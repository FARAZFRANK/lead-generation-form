== Installation ==

### Automatic Installation (Recommended)
1. Log in to your WordPress Admin Dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Plugins → Add New**.
3. Search for **"Lead Generation Form"** or **"Contact Form Builder"**.
4. Click **Install Now** on the Lead Generation Form plugin card.
5. Click **Activate**.
6. Go to **Lead Generation Form** in your admin menu to create your first form!

### Manual Installation
1. Download the plugin ZIP file from WordPress.org.
2. Log in to your WordPress dashboard and go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the ZIP file from your computer and click **Install Now**.
4. Click **Activate Plugin**.

### Embedding a Form on Your Website
* **Gutenberg Editor:** Insert a new block, search for **"Lead Generation Form"** (or type `/lead-form`), and choose your form from the dropdown.
* **Elementor:** Open your page in Elementor, search for the **"Lead Generation Form"** widget in the elements panel, and drag it into your section.
* **Shortcode:** Copy the shortcode `[WLFG id="1"]` from **Lead Generation Form → Manage Forms** and paste it into any page, post, or widget area.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is Lead Generation Form a free contact form builder? =
Yes! The free version provides a complete visual drag-and-drop form builder, pre-built form templates, unlimited lead storage, mini-CRM lead management, custom SMTP, Mailchimp integration, spam protection, and CSV export.

= Are there any limits on forms or leads in the Free version? =
No! You can create unlimited forms, add unlimited fields, and store unlimited leads in your WordPress database without any artificial caps.

= How do I create and embed a contact form? =
1. Go to **Lead Generation Form → Form Generator**.
2. Give your form a title and drag the fields you need (Name, Email, Message, Submit Button) onto the canvas, or click **Template Library** to select a pre-built template.
3. Click **Save Form**.
4. Copy the shortcode (e.g. `[WLFG id="1"]`) and paste it on any page, or use our native **Gutenberg Block** or **Elementor Widget**.

= Can I create multiple forms? =
Yes! You can create unlimited contact forms, lead capture forms, surveys, and booking forms. Each form gets its own unique ID and shortcode.

= How does the spam protection work? =
The plugin includes multi-layer anti-spam protection:
1. **Google reCAPTCHA v3:** Invisible bot detection without annoying puzzles.
2. **Honeypot Trap:** Invisible fields that trap automated spam bots.
3. **Timing Trap:** Rejects form submissions sent under 2 seconds (faster than any human).
4. **Email / Domain Blacklist & Turnstile (Pro):** Advanced disposable mailbox filtering, domain blocking, and Cloudflare Turnstile protection.

= Can I receive email notifications when a lead is captured? =
Yes! You can enable instant admin email notifications under **Settings → Email & SMTP**. You can use default WordPress mail or configure authenticated custom SMTP (with TLS or SSL encryption) to ensure your lead alerts never end up in the spam folder.

= Can I send an automatic confirmation email to the person who submitted the form? =
Yes! Enable the **Submitter Auto-Responder** in the Form Generator settings. You can customize the subject line and email body with dynamic tags such as `{Name}`, `{Email}`, `{Form_Name}`, and `{Submission_Date}`.

= Where are the captured leads stored? =
All form entries are saved directly in your WordPress database under **Lead Generation Form → All Captured Leads**. You can view, search, filter, update pipeline statuses (`New`, `Contacted`, `Won`, etc.), add internal sticky notes, and export them to CSV.

= Can I export my captured leads to Excel or CSV? =
Yes! Click **Export All Leads (CSV)** on the leads screen. The exported CSV file includes an Excel-friendly UTF-8 BOM encoding and built-in CSV formula injection protection.

= Does this plugin integrate with Mailchimp? =
Yes! You can connect your Mailchimp account via API key in **Settings → Newsletter & Integrations**. When visitors opt in, their contact information is automatically synchronized to your Mailchimp audience list with tag support.

= Can I customize the form design and colors? =
Yes! The built-in **Live Form Theme & Design Styler** lets you configure primary accent colors, border radius, dark mode, container card backgrounds, font families (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Outfit), field sizes, and button shadows per form.

= Is it compatible with Gutenberg and Elementor? =
Yes! The plugin features a dedicated Gutenberg Block (`wlgf/lead-form`) with live preview controls, a custom Elementor Widget (`wlgf_lead_form`), and universal shortcode support compatible with Divi, Beaver Builder, Astra, and all WordPress themes.

= What is the difference between Free and Pro? =
The Free version is ideal for contact forms, inquiry forms, lead capture, and surveys. **Lead Generation Form Pro** adds live online payments (Stripe & PayPal), dynamic math calculations & price estimators, 5-star rating & NPS matrix, multi-step wizard funnels, visual conditional logic, digital e-signatures, automated PDF receipts, multi-column grid layouts, outgoing webhooks, and extended email marketing connectors (Brevo, MailerLite, SendGrid, ActiveCampaign).
